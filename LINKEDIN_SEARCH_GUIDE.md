## 💡 PRO TIPS

### Tip 1: Filter by "Talked About" Topics
LinkedIn'de bazı kişilerin profile'ında "collateral", "margin call", "treasury operations" gibi keywords var. Bu keyword'leri LinkedIn Posts'ta search edebilirsin:

```
collateral management site:linkedin.com/posts
```

### Tip 2: Check "People Also Viewed"
Bir end user bulduğunda, profile'ın sağ tarafında "People also viewed" bölümü var. Genelde benzer role'deki kişiler çıkar.

### Tip 3: Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator (Free Trial)
Sales Navigator 30-day free trial var:
- Advanced filters (seniority, department, job function)
- Lead recommendations
- Unlimited searches

### Tip 4: Check Company Pages → "People" Tab
Her firma'nın LinkedIn company page'inde "People" tab'ı var. Orada:
1. Filter by: "Treasury", "Operations", "Collateral"
2. Scroll through results
3. Direct profile links

### Tip 5: Join LinkedIn Groups
Groups:
- "Treasury and Cash Management"
- "Collateral Management Professionals"
- "Repo Market Professionals"

Post your validation question in groups → organic engagement

## 🚨 RED FLAGS TO AVOID

❌ **Software Engineers** → Tech roles, not ops  
❌ **"Strategy" or "Innovation"** → Strategic validators, not end users  
❌ **"VP" or "Managing Director"** → Too senior, delegate to ops team  
❌ **"Consultant"** → External, not internal ops  
❌ **"Risk" or "Compliance"** → Adjacent but different workflow  

✅ **"Operations Manager"** → Perfect  
✅ **"Desk Manager" / "Desk Head"** → Perfect  
✅ **"Collateral Analyst"** → Good (junior but hands-on)  
✅ **"Middle Office"** → Perfect  
✅ **"Treasury Services"** → Good
